How it works

A request always knows where to go.

The org chart isn't just a diagram — it's the engine. When someone files a request, Kabuuan resolves the right approvers from your live structure and moves it along, so nothing sits in an inbox waiting for the wrong person.

STEP 01

Someone files it

An employee submits leave, overtime or any form from their own dashboard in a few taps.

STEP 02

It routes itself

The approval engine reads your org chart and sends it to the correct approver — automatically, even when roles change.

STEP 03

It's recorded

Once approved, balances update and HR gets a clean, exportable record. No re-keying, no chasing.
